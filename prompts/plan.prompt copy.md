## AI will build more software. Someone has to decide if it can be trusted.

Software delivery is moving from the **SDLC**, where people plan, build, and test in stages, to the **ADLC (Agentic Development Lifecycle)**: a continuous loop where AI agents build, test, and fix code while people direct and review.

More generated code means more decisions about trust. **Quality is no longer a final checkpoint. It is what makes AI speed usable for the business.**

---

## Every software role is changing. QA's role changes the most.

Developers, DevOps engineers, architects, tech leads, product owners, and business analysts are all becoming **AI Builders**: professionals who use AI to design, build, test, and maintain software within their own discipline.

For QA, this means moving from *executing tests* to *owning trust*: validating AI output, spotting critical risks, and building quality into every step of the lifecycle.
