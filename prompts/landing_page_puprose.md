# Recommended Landing Page Purpose:

### 1. Core Strategic Message

- We are not selling training. We are helping organizations transform their existing QA capabilities to deliver software faster, maintain quality, and adapt to AI-driven development — without relying on additional headcount.
- We avoid positioning the offering as a "course" or "training program." Instead, emphasize capability transformation, AI adoption, and business outcomes.
- Position the offering as a way to transform existing QA engineers professionals rather than hire additional testers.

### 2. AI-Driven Mindset & QA Transformation:

- The transformation requires more than learning how to use AI tools. It involves a fundamental shift in mindset and ways of working.
- AI tools should become an integral part of everyday software development and quality engineering processes.
- Traditional QA responsibilities are increasingly overlapping with those of developers and business analysts.
- While the boundaries of the QA role may become less distinct, quality assurance and quality ownership remain critical.
- Our offering should help organizations adapt to this evolution and redefine quality responsibilities in an AI-driven development lifecycle.

### 3. We follow EU Micro-Credentials & Recognition
-  EU-aligned micro-credentials as a way to formally recognize acquired AI and QA competencies.
-  Prefer developing our own clearly defined micro-credentials, with transparent descriptions of the skills, competencies, and assessment criteria they represent.
