This structure reflects the current `index.html` and follows `landing_page_puprose.md`.

## Positioning rules

- **We are not selling training.** We help organizations transform their existing QA capability to deliver software faster, maintain quality and adapt to AI-driven development — without relying on additional headcount.
- Avoid "course", "training program", "module", "learner", "seat" or "instructor" in visible copy. Use **capability transformation**, **engagement**, **approach**, **advisor** and **business outcomes** instead. The only exceptions are explicit contrasts ("Capability transformation, not training").
- Audience: **QA and engineering leaders** buying for their organization, not individuals buying for themselves.
- Message: **transform the QA engineers you already have** rather than hire additional testers.
- The transformation is a **mindset shift**, not just tool adoption. AI becomes part of everyday development and quality work.
- QA, development and business analysis responsibilities are converging. The boundaries blur, but **quality ownership** remains critical.
- Competence is recognized through our own **EU-aligned micro-credentials**, with transparent skills, competencies and assessment criteria.
- Do not invent advisor details, client results, ROI percentages or accreditation claims.

Quality Orchestration · Human-in-the-Loop · AI Development Lifecycle (ADLC) · Agentic AI · AI-Augmented QA · Risk-Based Testing · AI Evals · Continuous Quality · Critical Thinking · Quality Ownership · Self-Healing Test Automation

**H2:** Learning AI tools is the easy part. Changing how QA works is the transformation.

**Side text:** AI becomes part of everyday development and quality work, not an occasional add-on.

**Comparison table — From traditional QA to AI-Augmented QA**

| Dimension | Traditional QA | AI-Augmented QA |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Executes test cases and reports defects | **Quality Orchestration** — directs AI agents across the lifecycle |
| Timing | Tests at the end, before release | **Continuous Quality** — feedback at every step of the ADLC |
| Focus | Tries to cover everything equally | **Risk-Based Testing** — effort follows business impact |
| AI output | Trusted or ignored | **Human-in-the-Loop & Critical Thinking** — evaluated with **AI Evals** |
| Automation | Brittle scripts repaired by hand | **Self-Healing Test Automation** — agents propose fixes, QA approves |
| Responsibility | QA is a gate owned by one team | **Quality Ownership** — defined and shared across roles |

## 5. 03 / Quality ownership — `#role-shift`

**H2:** The boundaries of QA are blurring. Quality ownership is not.

**Side text:** QA, development and business analysis increasingly overlap. Someone still has to own the answer to "can we ship this?"

**Lede:** We help organizations redefine who owns which quality decisions in an AI-driven development lifecycle — and equip QA engineers to lead them.

**Role-evolution figure:**
1. *Overlapping responsibilities* — QA Engineers write code with AI agents · Developers generate tests with AI · Business Analysts validate requirements with AI · DevOps automate quality gates · Everyone uses AI every day

3. *Quality owner — AI-Augmented QA Engineer* — orchestrate agents · evaluate output · own quality.
