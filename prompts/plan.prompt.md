# AI Driven QA Engineering - Evaluate The role!
## Why now?
### AI will build more software. Make sure you can judge it.
### Delivery model changing. From SDLC to ADLC. From faster code to reliable commerce.
  - The Agentic Development Lifecycle (ADLC) is a new way of building software. It’s a continuous loop of AI agents building, testing, and fixing code while people direct and review.
  - ADLC is a shift from the traditional Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC), where people plan, build, and test in stages.
    
### Roles are changing
  - Software roles are moving toward the AI Software Builder model.
  - Software Developers, Software Testers,DevOps Engineers, Architects, Technical Leads, Product Owners, Business Analysts - AI Builder roles are emerging in every software discipline. The AI Builder is a professional who uses AI to design, develop, test, optimize, and maintain software within their area of expertise.
