01 / Why now

AI builds software faster.
Your QA capability has to keep pace.

SDLC — Software Development Lifecycle: people plan, build and test in stages.

ADLC — AI Development Lifecycle: agentic AI builds, tests and fixes code in a continuous loop while people direct, review and decide.

More generated code means more decisions about trust. Quality is no longer a final checkpoint. Continuous quality is what makes AI speed usable for the business.

SDLC — Sequential delivery → ADLC — Continuous quality

AI speed in development needs AI speed in QA. Hiring more testers does not scale with AI-generated code — transforming the QA engineers you already have does.
02 / Mindset shift

Learning AI tools is the easy part.
Changing how QA works is the transformation.

AI becomes part of everyday development and quality work, not an occasional add-on. That needs a new mindset as well as new skills.

03 / Quality ownership

The boundaries of QA are blurring.
Quality ownership is not.

QA, development and business analysis increasingly overlap. Someone still has to own the answer to “can we ship this?”

AI lets developers generate tests, analysts validate requirements and QA engineers write code with agents. We help your organization redefine who owns which quality decisions in an AI-driven development lifecycle — and equip your QA engineers to lead them.
